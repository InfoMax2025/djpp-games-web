GIN RUMMY ♠♥♦♣

Big cards, easy play, a fair deal. Play offline against the opponent.
Goal: be first to 100 points over several hands.
Each turn: take one card from the stock or discard pile, then tap one card to throw away. That's the whole turn.
Melds stay in your hand: runs (7♥8♥9♥) and sets (three of a kind) are kept — we outline them in gold and leave them out of your deadwood. You don't play them; they lay down when you knock.
Deadwood: the points in your leftover, un-melded cards (face cards = 10, ace = 1). We total it for you.
Knock: when your deadwood is 10 or less you can knock to end the hand. Gin = every card melded (0 deadwood) for a bonus.
Honest: the shuffle is truly random and the opponent only sees what you see — no peeking, no rigged hands, no ads.
