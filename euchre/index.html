Partner tips
Goal: you + your partner (top) race the two opponents to 10 points.
Bowers: the Jack of trump is the Right Bower (highest). The other Jack of the same color is the Left Bower (2nd, and it counts as trump).
Each hand: call or pass on trump, then play 5 tricks. Follow the led suit if you can. Highest trump — or highest card of the led suit — wins the trick.
Scoring: take 3–4 tricks = 1, all 5 = 2, alone & all 5 = 4. Call it and take fewer than 3 = "euchred," opponents get 2.
Options: Stick the dealer — if everyone passes twice the dealer must call. Defend alone (Canadian loner) — when someone goes alone, one defender may go alone too; euchre them and it's worth 4. Farmer's hand — dealt three 9s/10s? Swap them for the three buried cards. Partner tips — your partner says why it played each card.
Honest: deals are truly random, the computer plays by the same rules you do — no rigged hands, no timers, no ads.
