CRIBBAGE ♠♥♦♣

Big cards. Every point counted for you, out loud. A fair deal, always.
Goal: race your pegs down the board to 121 points.
Each hand: you get 6 cards — keep 4, send 2 to the crib (a bonus hand the dealer scores).
The play: take turns laying cards without going over 31. Fifteens, pairs and runs score as you go.
The count: then every hand is counted for you, step by step — fifteen-two, fifteen-four… nothing missed, no math needed.
Honest: truly random shuffles. The opponent only ever sees its own cards — and every count is shown so you can check it.
