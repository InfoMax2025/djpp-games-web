Privacy Policy — Control Tower 3D: ATC Sim

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Control Tower 3D: ATC Sim is published by DJ's Puzzle Palace ("we").

Data we collect

None. Control Tower 3D does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or device data. The app has no account system, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking.

Local storage

Your scores, statistics, settings and any saved shift are stored only on your own device. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Radio voices

Pilot radio calls are spoken with your device's own text-to-speech engine. No audio is recorded, and nothing is sent to us.

Motion sensors

If you turn on gyro look, the app reads your device's orientation sensor to move the tower view. This reading is used on the spot and is never stored or transmitted.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
